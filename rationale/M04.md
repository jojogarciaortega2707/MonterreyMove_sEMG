# Rationale — M04 (new data integration for trials)

I support recording 6 test sessions on 3 different days. Our 11 sessions come from one continuous recording of 20 minutes, so they are almost one condition. A new day brings a new electrode placement, new skin and a new baseline. My doubt is the script start. Until the app logs it, we write the computer clock by hand, with about 1 s of error, so the first second of each block is dropped. I also doubt that placement stays the same between days. We follow the SENIAM midpoint, measure 2 cm and take one photo per session to check it.

Source: Hermens, H. J., Freriks, B., Disselhorst-Klug, C., & Rau, G. (2000). Development of recommendations for SEMG sensors and sensor placement procedures. *Journal of Electromyography and Kinesiology*, 10(5), 361–374.
