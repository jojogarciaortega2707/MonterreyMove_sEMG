# Rationale — M03 (data cleaning)

I support rejecting single seconds instead of whole sessions. The flat runs in the raw signal last 51 to 601 samples, always a multiple of 50, so they are lost packets, not muscle. Rule R1 removes them and keeps the rest of the session: only 22 of 1,225 seconds were rejected. My doubt is rule R2. We do not know the converter limit in µV, so "the session extreme repeated 3 times" is only a proxy for saturation. The rules were also written after a first model run. That is why they are frozen now and are applied unchanged to the test sessions.

Source: De Luca, C. J., Gilmore, L. D., Kuznetsov, M., & Roy, S. H. (2010). Filtering the surface EMG signal: Movement artifact and baseline noise contamination. *Journal of Biomechanics*, 43(8), 1573–1579.
