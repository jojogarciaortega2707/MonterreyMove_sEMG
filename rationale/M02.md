# Rationale — M02 (feature identification)

After further researching, I support the use of the seven features of one 1-s window: the log of the mean, maximum, minimum and SD, and three ratios. They use only the same window, so nothing leaks from thresholds or game counters which we also mention on our quality report. 

Source: Phinyomark, A., Phukpattaranont, P., & Limsakul, C. (2012). Feature reduction and selection for EMG signal classification. *Expert Systems with Applications*, 39(8), 7420–7431.
