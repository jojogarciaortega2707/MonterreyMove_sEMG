# Rationale — M02 (feature identification)

I support the seven features of one 1-s window: the log of the mean, maximum, minimum and SD, and three ratios. They use only the same window, so nothing leaks from thresholds or game counters 

Source: Phinyomark, A., Phukpattaranont, P., & Limsakul, C. (2012). Feature reduction and selection for EMG signal classification. *Expert Systems with Applications*, 39(8), 7420–7431.
