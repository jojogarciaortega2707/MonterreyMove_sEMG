# Rationale — M02 (feature identification)

I support the seven features of one 1-s window: the log of the mean, maximum, minimum and SD, and three ratios. They use only the same window, so nothing leaks from thresholds or game counters. My doubt is that four of them are amplitude, and the label is amplitude too. Without amplitude, the shape ratios reach F1 0.848, which is closer to the real difficulty. A 1-s summary also hides the waveform. Next I would use 250 ms windows of the raw 2000 Hz signal with MAV, WL, ZC, SSC and MDF, which are standard for sEMG.

Source: Phinyomark, A., Phukpattaranont, P., & Limsakul, C. (2012). Feature reduction and selection for EMG signal classification. *Expert Systems with Applications*, 39(8), 7420–7431.
