# Rationale — M01 (machine learning model and validation)

At  the beginning I had no experience on ML, however after learning with the AI tools and researching about a Model that could help us categorizing between "contraction" and "rest". I learned about the technique "leave-one-session-out" validation and the frozen model. In this scenario, the session is used to train and to test in the same fold, and the model, k and T were fixed on 2026-11-20 before any test session. With this we were able to categorize between rest, contraction, artifacts and some in between values. 

Source: Varoquaux, G. (2018). Cross-validation failure: small sample sizes lead to large error bars. *NeuroImage*, 180, 68–77.
