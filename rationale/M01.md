# Rationale — M01 (machine learning model and validation)

I support leave-one-session-out validation and the frozen model. No session is used to train and to test in the same fold, and the model, k and T were fixed on 2026-11-20 before any test session. My doubt is our F1 of 1.000. The labels come from an amplitude rule, so the model only copies that rule. With 11 sessions from one morning, the error bars are also wide. I trust only the sealed test on other days, with labels from the calibration script. If a threshold is as good there, we keep the threshold.

Source: Varoquaux, G. (2018). Cross-validation failure: small sample sizes lead to large error bars. *NeuroImage*, 180, 68–77.
